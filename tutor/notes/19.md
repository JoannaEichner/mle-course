## What the learner should own after this module

- Evidence in order: baseline on the same rows, backtest per fold, one look at the test, cost.
- Results with their spread (per-fold numbers), not a single mean; a difference is believed when it repeats fold by fold.
- The test set described before it is read: weather, promotions, stockouts. A test that contradicts the backtest is explained, not "fixed".
- A monitoring plan: weekly WAPE and bias against the baseline, input drift, a retraining rhythm, a way back to the previous model.
- A report that a stranger can check: every number traceable to MLflow, `test_scores.csv` or a commit; limitations stated by the author.

## Facts worth having at hand

Standard profile (city 3); the notebook's backtest uses the configuration's 4 folds of 7 days ending on the last day of `train.parquet` (2024-05-29 to 06-25), the test is `eval.parquet` (2024-06-26 to 07-02).

- Backtest, in-stock WAPE mean (bias): seasonal naive 0.562 (−0.036), LightGBM 400 trees 0.377 (+0.038), GRU 0.390 (−0.046). Per fold: LightGBM 0.395, 0.394, 0.358, 0.361; GRU 0.407, 0.393, 0.382, 0.379; naive 0.572, 0.570, 0.558, 0.548.
- Test week: mean temperature 28.6 °C; 93.8% of training days are colder. Promotion days 33.7% in training, 37.3% in the test week; in-stock days 59.0% and 59.6%.
- Test scores from `freshcast evaluate` (LightGBM from the pipeline): in-stock WAPE 0.371 (bias +0.020) against 0.517 (bias −0.082) for seasonal naive; all days 0.538 against 0.608.
- Full profile (design doc): test week seasonal naive 0.389, LightGBM 0.285 in-stock; GRU 0.308 against LightGBM 0.306 in the backtest.
- `evaluate` reads `forecast.parquet`, so the order is `train`, `forecast`, `evaluate`. A learner who runs `evaluate` first gets `FileNotFoundError` for `forecast.parquet`.
- Check 19.4 is ungraded and checks form only: the five section headings (Dane, Walidacja, Wyniki, Wybór modelu, Ograniczenia), at least six decimal numbers and the word WAPE. Content is judged in `/review`.

## Tickets

### 19.1 `compare_models`

- A typo in `fit_masks` silently training a model on all rows: the check expects `ValueError` for a mask of an unknown model.
- Different folds per model, or scoring the GRU on in-stock rows only while LightGBM on all: the backtest function makes it the same by construction; if they write their own loop, ask them to show the fold dates of every model.

### 19.2 `seasonal_naive_forecast`, `test_scores`

- Seasonal naive for the test days taken from the same test days (a shift inside the test frame): leakage, and the seventh day is missing. The docstring requires the history.
- Filling a missing week-earlier value with zero: the check expects an error.
- Scoring model and baseline on different rows.

### 19.3 `evaluate`

- Computing the forecast inside `evaluate` instead of reading `forecast.parquet`: works, but then the evaluated forecast is not the one delivered. Ask which forecast the shop would have used.
- Running `evaluate` several times while changing the model: C5. Ask how many times they looked at the test and what they changed in between; the report must say it.

## The report: what a good one contains

- **Dane.** 2,648 series in one city (or 50,000 on full), 90 days, the zero-discount decision, in-stock training; the test week hotter than 94% of training days, with slightly more promotions.
- **Walidacja.** Rolling origin, 4 × 7 days, training on in-stock days, scoring in-stock WAPE with bias, the test seen once. Why not a random split (module 11).
- **Wyniki.** A table with the three models per fold and mean, and the test row for the chosen model next to the baseline.
- **Wybór modelu.** LightGBM: better or equal in every fold, cheaper, no GPU needed. The GRU is close and a candidate for an ensemble; the full profile narrows the gap.
- **Ograniczenia.** One city (on standard), one test week, a hot one; stockout days excluded from training, so bias can drift; no prices; new products without history; the monitoring plan.

Weak reports show a single number, call results "good" without a comparison, or tune after seeing the test. Ask for the per-fold table first; most other problems show up there.
